About

Amethyst is a multilingual “4 Pic 1 Answer” puzzle game. Look at four pictures, find the answer they have in common, then trace it through the letter grid in one swipe.

Every puzzle is the one word the four pictures share, shown in four different ways. Short answers come quickly. The long ones take some thought.

The game is simple and uncluttered. There is nothing to buy and no energy timer. The ads are a small banner and a rewarded video you can choose to watch. There are no full-screen ads between puzzles and no forced videos.

The app does not require an account and stores all progress on your device. Read the Privacy Policy.
