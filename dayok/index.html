About

A calm calendar and clock for your living-room TV or tablet. Day OK shows the next three days, the week, the month and the year from the Google Calendar already on your device, in large type you can read from the sofa.

It reads your calendar and never changes your events: it cannot add, change or delete them. There is no weather, no news, no photos and no ads. You do not create an account or type a password: your calendars sync through the device, as they already do.

Pay once. No subscription, no in-app purchases, nothing to unlock.

Features

  • 3-day, week, month and year views, switched with one button on the remote
  • A big clock and date, in your language and your 12- or 24-hour format
  • Colours per calendar; choose which calendars to show
  • Event details: time, place, repetition, people, description
  • Search your events
  • Night clock for the bedroom or the hallway
  • Keep the screen on: off, always, or only at the times you choose
  • A calm moving background that follows the seasons, or none
  • Light and dark themes; a choice of easy-to-read fonts
  • English and Romanian

Private by design

  • The app has no internet permission: it cannot send anything anywhere.
