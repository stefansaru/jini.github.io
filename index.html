About

Amethyst is a multilingual “4 Pic 1 Answer” puzzle game. Look at four pictures, find the answer they have in common, then trace it through the letter grid in one swipe.

Each puzzle shows the same answer in four different ways - an animal, place, tool, action, object, or idea. Some answers are quick and easy; longer ones offer a tougher challenge.

Amethyst is designed to be simple, accessible, and uncluttered. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or energy timers. Ads are limited to a small banner and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch. There are no full-screen ads between puzzles and no forced videos.

We respect your privacy. The app does not require an account and stores all progress locally on your device. Read our Privacy Policy.
