Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-17 · applies to the Android app (Google Play) and the iOS app (App Store)

Amethyst ("we", "our", "the app") is a free word-puzzle game. This page explains what data is collected when you use the app and how it is used. The same app, with the same privacy behaviour, is published for Android on Google Play and for iPhone and iPad on the App Store; where the two platforms differ, this page says so.

Data controller

The data controller responsible for this app is:
Jini Solutions
An independent studio run by a sole developer, who is the data controller as a natural person
Bucharest, Romania
Contact: hello@jini.solutions

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information directly. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, or any other identifying information, and does not transmit any gameplay data to our servers.

All progress (completed puzzles, settings, hint counts, your declared age band) is stored exclusively on your device — in the app's private local storage on Android, in the app's sandbox on iOS — and is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes it; so does Settings → Delete all local data inside the app.

Voluntary defect reporting. If you choose to report a technical issue, you may send an email to hello@jini.solutions. To help us investigate and resolve the issue, we recommend including some or all of the following — the choice of what to share is entirely yours:

  • Phone model
  • Phone operating system version (Android or iOS)
  • Your Amethyst version and in-app settings
  • Error logs captured by the app
  • Screenshots or screen recordings showing the malfunction

This information is used solely to diagnose and resolve the reported defect. It reaches us through the jini.solutions mail forwarding service and is kept in the controller's own mailbox; it is not shared with any third party. You may request deletion of the email thread at any time by replying to it; please be aware that deletion is carried out immediately upon request but will permanently remove the correspondence and may prevent us from completing the investigation.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising network operated by Google LLC, on both platforms: one small banner, and a rewarded video you choose to watch. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalised or non-personalised ads, including:

  • Device identifiers — the Advertising ID (GAID) on Android; on iOS the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), which AdMob can read only if you have allowed tracking (see Apple platform services below)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage and interaction data
  • Device model, OS version, language, and time zone

This data is collected and processed by Google under their own privacy policy. You can review it at policies.google.com/privacy.

Legal basis (EU/EEA/UK users): processing by AdMob is based on your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). On first launch you will be shown a consent dialogue powered by Google's User Messaging Platform, on Android and on iOS alike. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from Top right corner → Manage ad consent.

If you decline personalised advertising, the app will display non-personalised ads instead and all features remain fully available.

Google platform services (Android)

On Android the app uses Google Play Services for standard functionality. Google Play Services may collect certain device and usage information as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Apple platform services (iOS)

On iPhone and iPad the app runs on Apple's platform services. Apple may collect device and usage information as described in Apple's Privacy Policy. The app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt is never shown), so the Identifier for Advertisers is not available to AdMob unless you have allowed tracking for all apps in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can also turn off personalised ads from Apple in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising. The App Store listing carries the app's privacy "nutrition label", which summarises this page.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. On first launch the app asks which age band you are in; a player who declares 17 or younger is never shown the personalisation dialogue and receives non-personalised ads only. If you believe your child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at hello@jini.solutions and we will take prompt action to remove it.

Data retention

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, there is no data held by us to retain or delete. Game progress stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app or choose Settings → Delete all local data. For data held by Google (AdMob / Play Services), refer to Google's data retention policies; for data held by Apple, to Apple's.

Your rights (EU / EEA / UK — GDPR)

Because the app keeps everything on your device and we hold no copy, you can exercise most rights yourself, at once, without asking us:

  • Delete everything the app knows: uninstall the app, or open Settings → Delete all local data. Both remove your progress, settings, history and age band completely; nothing survives on our side, because nothing ever left the device.
  • If you have emailed us: your message and anything you attached is the only data we hold. Reply to that email and ask us to delete the conversation. We delete it and stop working on it; that ends the exchange.
  • Advertising data (the advertising identifier, ad interactions) is held by Google, not by us. Withdraw or limit consent in the app at Top right corner → Manage ad consent; for Google's own records use the request tool linked below.

Your rights (California — CCPA)

California residents may opt out of the sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. To do so:

  • Use the in-app consent control: Top right corner → Manage ad consent (select non-personalised ads).
  • On Android, adjust your device-level ad preferences via Settings → Google → Ads.
  • On iOS, use Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Manage Google ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

We do not discriminate against users who exercise their CCPA rights. All app features are equally available regardless of ad consent choice.
